GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start.

Safety first

Move to a safe place and switch on hazard lights.

Don't move vehicles blindly

Follow local rules about moving vehicles after a crash.

Keep your details

Note names, plates and contacts of those involved.

INJURIES OR DANGER?

Call emergency services first.

If anyone is hurt or there is immediate danger, call 999 or 112 before requesting help here. For a breakdown with no accident, use roadside assistance.
