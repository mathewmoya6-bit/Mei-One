HOW IT WORKS

From application to your first request.

Three steps, and the first takes a few minutes.

01 01

Apply online

Tell us who you are, where you work and what you offer. The form is short.

02 02

We review it

The MEI team checks your details and contacts you on the phone number or email you gave. We may ask for supporting documents.

03 03

Get set up

Approved partners are onboarded and start receiving requests from MEI One customers.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Have these ready.

You only need a few details.

  • A phone number and email we can reach you on
  • The town or area where you work
  • A short description of what you offer: vehicle, tools, trade or products
  • For drivers and towing: your driving licence and vehicle details. We ask for documents after the first review, never in the application form.
  • For garages, sellers and home services: business details or examples of your work, if you have them
QUESTIONS

Short answers.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free. Terms for partners are shared with you during onboarding.

Can I apply for more than one service?

Yes. Submit one application for each service, for example one as a driver and one as a roadside responder.

Do I need an MEI One account?

Not to apply. If you sign in first, you can follow the status of your applications on the application page.

How do you use my details?

Only to review your application and contact you about it. We ask for your agreement before you submit.

How will I hear back?

The MEI team contacts you using the phone number or email on your application.
