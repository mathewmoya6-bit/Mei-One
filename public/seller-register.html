APPLICATION PROCESS

What happens next?

  • Sign in to your MEI One account.
  • Submit your shop details and applicable documents.
  • Our team reviews the application.
  • Once approved, you can set up products, stock and retail or wholesale prices.
  • Your approved shop and active products can then appear in the marketplace.

Before you apply

Use a business phone number you can access. Make sure document photos are readable and show all edges of the document.
