BEFORE YOU APPLY

A few details to prepare

Having this information ready can make your application easier.

  • A working phone number and email address.
  • The town, county or area where you operate.
  • A short description of your service, vehicle, equipment, trade or products.
  • For drivers and towing operators: relevant driving licence and vehicle information. Supporting documents may be requested during review.
  • For garages, sellers and home service professionals: business details, relevant experience or examples of your work, where available.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What you may want to know

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Submitting a partner application is free. Any applicable partner terms, fees or commercial arrangements will be explained during onboarding before you decide whether to proceed.

Can I apply for more than one service?

Yes. Submit a separate application for each service category you want to offer, such as driving and roadside assistance.

Do I need an MEI One account to apply?

You can submit an application without signing in. Account features and application-status access depend on the options available in the application portal.

Will my application be approved automatically?

No. Applications are reviewed. The team may request more information or supporting documents before making an onboarding decision.

How will MEI One contact me?

Our team will use the phone number or email address you provide in your application. Please make sure your contact details are accurate and active.

Does applying guarantee customers or jobs?

No. Applying or being onboarded does not guarantee a particular number of customer requests, bookings, sales or earnings.

How will my information be used?

Your application information is used to assess your application and communicate with you about the partner process. Please review the privacy information presented in the application before submitting your details.
